🏁 Doom Riders - Race Position Display Added! 🏁

✅ Game Over Screen Now Shows Race Position!

What You'll See After Racing:

  • Final Score: Your total points earned
  • Money Earned: Session money from the race
  • NEW: Race Position: Your finishing place in the race!
  • High Score: Best score achieved

Position Display Colors:

  • 🥇 1st Place: Gold text
  • 🥈 2nd Place: Silver text
  • 🥉 3rd Place: Bronze text
  • 🏃 4th+ Place: White text

How It Works:

When you finish a race (or crash out), your final position among all racers is recorded and displayed prominently on the Game Over screen. The position is locked when you finish - other racers finishing after you won't change your recorded position!
